How to play

  • Type a whole-number answer and press Enter or tap Answer.
  • A correct answer earns 10 points, plus a streak bonus of up to 10 extra points.
  • A wrong answer costs 2 points; skipping costs 1. Your score never drops below zero.
  • Every 5 correct answers raises the difficulty. You have 60 seconds—press Play again after the round ends.
