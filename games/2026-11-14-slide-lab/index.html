How to play

  1. Choose a 3 × 3, 4 × 4, or 5 × 5 board.
  2. Press Start puzzle to begin the timer.
  3. Move a tile beside the empty space until the numbers are in order.
  4. Use Undo to take back your last move. Your timer keeps running.

Keyboard: Arrow keys or W A S D move the empty space. Your best result is saved locally for each board size when browser storage is available.
